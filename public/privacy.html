Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run this site. They process data only on our instructions:

  • Web3Forms and FormSubmit — delivery of form submissions to our inbox
  • The Payment Economics (hosted on Substack) — newsletter subscriptions
  • Google Fonts — web fonts (your IP address is sent to Google when fonts load)
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting
  • Scaleway (Paris region) — storage of early-access requests
  • Oryjin's own application (app.oryjin.com, hosted by Scaleway, Paris region) — the sales desk your early-access request is filed on, with the campaign source it arrived with
  • HubSpot (EU data centre) — the CRM where our sales team follows up on requests
  • PostHog (EU Cloud, Frankfurt) — site analytics, only with your consent
  • LinkedIn and Google — advertising measurement tags, only with your consent and only while a campaign runs

Some of these providers are located outside the European Union. Where this is the case, transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Early-access applications are kept for up to three years after our last exchange, then deleted. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or export your data, object to its use, or withdraw consent at any time by writing to contact@oryjin.com. We reply within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Cookies and analytics

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept two kinds of cookies. Nothing in either category is set until you choose, and refusing is one click, like accepting.

  • Analytics — PostHog, hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt). It records the pages you view, the links you click, where you came from and the kind of device you use, under a random identifier. Session replay is off on this site. The same identifier is shared with app.oryjin.com, so that if you later open an account we can see which pages led you there.
  • Advertising — the LinkedIn Insight Tag and the Google Ads conversion tag, which tell those platforms that a visit or an early-access request came from one of our campaigns. They load only while a campaign runs.

Your choice is kept in a cookie named oryjin_consent for six months, after which the banner asks again. You can change it at any time from the “Cookies” link in the footer of every page. Strictly necessary storage (the form's session memory of the campaign you arrived from) needs no consent and holds no identifier.

An early-access request is filed on Oryjin's sales desk in our application and in our CRM (HubSpot, EU data centre), together with the campaign it arrived from, so that the person who answers you knows the context. It is never sold or shared with advertisers.
