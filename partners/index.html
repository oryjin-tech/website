How the analysis works

A live workspace —
not a report you wait for.

Oryjin isn't a consulting engagement that ends in a deck. You get an account, you log in, and the analysis is visible while it happens.

You log in and watch it build
Your workspace is live from day one. As each document is read, the fee breakdown, effective rates and discrepancies appear in it — you're looking at the same view we are.
Analysis starts when documents land
There's no queue and no batch window. A settlement report uploaded today is being reconciled against your contract terms straight away.
The picture stays current
Providers reprice quietly. Every new invoice is checked as it arrives, so drift from your contract surfaces when it happens — not at the next review.
The pace is set by your data, not our calendar — the more complete the documents, the sharper the picture. Findings surface continuously as coverage grows, so there's no single date to wait for.

Choose your format

However you want to
work with us.

Design partners aren't on a fixed program. Pick the shape that fits how your team works — and change your mind later if it turns out another one fits better.

Option A
Snapshot audit
One reconciliation on the documents you already have on file. The lowest-commitment way in: you see what Oryjin finds on real history before deciding anything else.
Option B
Always-on
Documents flow in as your providers produce them and the workspace stays current. This is where Oryjin is strongest — it catches pricing drift as it happens, and it's the setup that teaches us the most.
Option C
Shaped around you
A specific provider you're about to renegotiate, one entity, one market, one quarter you're suspicious about. Tell us the question you actually want answered and we'll scope it to that.
Not sure which one? Start with the snapshot audit — it's the fastest way to see whether there's anything worth chasing, and moving to always-on afterwards costs you nothing.

Your data

What to send —
and what to never send.

Oryjin works on commercial documents, not on payment data. The distinction matters, and it keeps the whole engagement out of scope for the parts of compliance nobody enjoys.

What we need
  • ✓Provider contracts and pricing annexes — including amendments and renegotiated terms.
  • ✓Invoices from your PSPs, acquirers and orchestrators.
  • ✓Settlement and fee reports — the monthly exports your providers already generate.
  • ✓Whatever format they arrive in. PDF, CSV, XLSX, an ugly export nobody has opened in a year — all fine.
  • ✓Optional — read-only API access. If you'd rather not gather files by hand, you can generate a restricted key in your provider dashboard: reporting endpoints only, revocable by you whenever you want. Entirely optional — documents alone work just as well.
What we never want
  • ✕Card data. No PANs, no expiry dates, no CVVs — in any file, ever.
  • ✕Your customers' personal data. Names, emails, addresses, order details. If an export contains them, strip the columns or tell us and we'll tell you which ones to drop.
  • ✕Passwords, or keys we didn't ask for. Never your dashboard password, never a key with write access — nothing that can move money or change settings. The only credential we'd ever ask for is the read-only reporting key you generate yourself. If anyone asks you for more than that in our name, it isn't us.
Hosted in the EU. Your documents are used solely to produce your own analysis — never shared with third parties, never used to benchmark you to another client by name. They're kept for the duration of the engagement, and deleted on request at any time — write to contact@oryjin.com and we'll confirm once it's done. French law, GDPR-aligned.
