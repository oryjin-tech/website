Everything you need to know

You drag and drop the documents you already have — pricing contracts, invoices, transaction reports and internal order extracts. Oryjin reads and files each document automatically. No IT project, nothing to install, nothing to ask from your providers.

Oryjin is built for the modern payment stack — PSPs, orchestrators and acquirers. Today that includes deep, line-level support for providers like Stripe and Adyen, alongside pricing contracts, invoices and order extracts from any source — and coverage expands continuously with our early-access partners.

A finding is a gap Oryjin detected between what your contracts promise and what your providers actually charged. Each finding shows the expected amount, the billed amount and the variance in euros, is classified as Recoverable, Needs review or Not recoverable, and carries its full evidence trail across runs — so every claim you file is backed.

Continuously. Runs start automatically as documents arrive, and you can trigger one at any time. Results and alerts are delivered straight to the channels your team already watches.

Your data is hosted in the EU (Scaleway, Paris region) and handled in line with GDPR. Access is scoped per organisation and company with role-based permissions — your documents are used only for your own reconciliation, never shared, never used to train anything.
