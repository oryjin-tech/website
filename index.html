Payment fee questions, answered

Stripe and Adyen through connectors that keep pulling settlements, fees and authorisations. Any other PSP or acquirer (Mangopay, Worldline, Checkout.com, PayPal, Braintree and others) is added from the contracts, invoices and reports you already have.

Your PSP shows its own fees. Oryjin shows the full cost of a payment across every provider, including FX, fraud tooling, chargebacks and refunds, checks it against what your contracts say, explains what moved it, and forecasts next quarter.

Detected euros were billed above your contract, backed by payment references your PSP can check. Modelled euros are estimates, such as what interchange++ pricing could save, and each states its assumptions. The two are never added together.

Oryjin is not a PSP and not an orchestrator: it never moves money, never changes a setting, and has no stake in which provider you use. Data is hosted in the EU by Scaleway (Paris), never includes cardholder data, and is covered by an NDA before sharing and a DPA from day one.

Early access is a paid 90-day design partnership on your real data, and its fee is credited to your first year. Oryjin is then a yearly subscription priced by the monthly payment volume it covers, with unlimited users and no success fee.
